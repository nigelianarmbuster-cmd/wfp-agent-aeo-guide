    Answer Engines & Evaluation Method

      Metric Definitions

      • Visibility — share of the tracked queries captured in that period in which {{subject}} appeared anywhere in the answer's citations.
      • Average Rank — mean position of {{subject}} among citations, counting only queries where {{subject}} appeared. 1 = first citation; lower is better.
      • Citation Share — {{subject}} appearances as a percentage of all citations surfaced across the tracked queries.
      • Recommendation Rate — share of queries in which the engine recommended {{subject}} (a {{subject}} property, agent, or office).
      • Share of Voice — {{subject}} entity mentions versus mentions of the tracked competitors, across all responses.
      • Per-engine figures — each engine is asked the same query set; engine-level metrics are computed over that engine's captured rows for the period.

      Rank Convention & Dataset

      Rank is the position of {{subject}} in the answer's citations for a given query: 1 = first citation. Ranks above 7 are shown as 7. Queries where {{subject}} was not cited are excluded from Average Rank and shown as a grey dash in the heatmap.
